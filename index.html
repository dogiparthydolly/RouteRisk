How it works
Your route. Our radar.

Paste your route

Drop in any Strava or RideWithGPS URL — or describe where you're riding. RouteRisk reads the route name, terrain, and region.

AI maps the hazards

Cross-references terrain data, road surface records, known wildlife zones, seasonal patterns, and historical incident data for the region.

You ride smarter

Get a section-by-section breakdown — gravel patches, blind descents, cattle grids, animal crossing probability, and weather risk windows.

Disclaimer: RouteRisk is an AI-powered planning tool, not a certified safety system. Hazard assessments are probabilistic and based on general terrain, surface, and wildlife data — they do not reflect real-time conditions. The model can make mistakes. Road surfaces change, animals are unpredictable, and weather evolves. Always ride within your ability, wear appropriate safety gear, and verify conditions locally before you set out. RouteRisk accepts no liability for incidents on routes it has analyzed.
